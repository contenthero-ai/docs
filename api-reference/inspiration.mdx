Base URL `https://app.contenthero.ai/api/v1`. Every endpoint takes a bearer API key. The table below is generated from the route contract: the Scope column is the scope the key needs, and `None` means any valid key. Starting or stopping tracking needs `inspiration:write`, and `POST /content/{id}/analysis` spends credits and needs `studio:generate`.

## Accounts and content

`GET /accounts` returns both kinds of tracked account; narrow it with `accountType` (`inspiration` or `brand`) and `brandKitId`. `GET /content` ranks posts across the creators you watch and your own accounts; `scope` picks which. On `GET /content/{id}`, the transcript (`transcript`, windowed with `startMs` and `endMs`), the Break It Down (`analysis`) and the scenes (`scenes=map`: each scene's range, what happens and what is said; a scene's frames come from `view`) are opt-in, and their availability always reports. `POST /content/{id}/analysis` makes a post's Break It Down (`kind: "breakdown"`, the default), its scenes (`kind: "scenes"`) or its video copies (`kind: "video"`). It is free when the result exists and otherwise charged once when stored; pass `getCost: true` to see the price first.

| Method | Path | Scope | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/accounts` | None | The social accounts you track, both kinds (`accountType`: `inspiration` or `brand`), a page at a time. |
| `POST` | `/accounts` | `inspiration:write` | Start tracking accounts. |
| `GET` | `/accounts/{id}` | None | One tracked account with its performance: totals, averages, top and recent content. |
| `PATCH` | `/accounts/{id}` | `inspiration:write` | Change a tracked account's placement in your lists. |
| `DELETE` | `/accounts/{id}` | `inspiration:write` | Stop tracking an account. |
| `GET` | `/content` | None | Tracked posts, ranked by outlier score by default, across the creators you watch and your own accounts (`scope`). |
| `GET` | `/content/{id}` | None | One tracked post in full: engagement, outlier score, and on request its transcript and analysis. |
| `POST` | `/content/{id}/analysis` | `studio:generate` | Analyze a post: its Break It Down (`kind: "breakdown"`, the default), its scenes, or its video. Pass `getCost` to price it first. |

## The mining pattern

Outliers come back shallow (title and metrics). To draft from a pattern, list and rank with `/content`, then get the depth (transcript, hashtags) with `/content/{id}` on the few you chose. See [Voice synthesis](/skills/voice-synthesis) for the method and the [Inspiration guide](/guides/inspiration) for the workflow.
