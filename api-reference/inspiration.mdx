Base URL `https://app.contenthero.ai/api/v1`. Every endpoint takes a bearer API key. `POST /content/{id}/analysis` spends credits and needs `studio:generate`.

## Accounts and content

| Method | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/accounts` | The social accounts you track, both kinds. Filter with `account_type` (`inspiration` or `brand`) and `brand_kit_id`. |
| `GET` | `/accounts/{id}` | One account with its performance: totals, averages, top and recent content. |
| `GET` | `/content` | Posts, ranked by outlier score, across the creators you watch and your own accounts (`scope`). Every row carries `isOwn`. |
| `GET` | `/content/{id}` | One post in full. Transcript (`transcript`, windowed with `start_ms`/`end_ms`), Break It Down (`analysis`) and scenes (`scenes=map` or `frames`) are opt-in; their availability always reports. |
| `POST` | `/content/{id}/analysis` | Make a post's Break It Down (`kind: "breakdown"`, the default) or its scenes (`kind: "scenes"`). Free when it exists; otherwise charged once when stored. Pass `getCost: true` to see the price first. |

## The mining pattern

Outliers come back shallow (title and metrics). To draft from a pattern, list and rank with `/content`, then get the depth (transcript, hashtags) with `/content/{id}` on the few you chose. See [Voice synthesis](/skills/voice-synthesis) for the method and the [Inspiration guide](/guides/inspiration) for the workflow.
