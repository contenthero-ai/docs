The pipeline is the hero workflow. It takes a card from research to publication: ground it in your inspiration outliers and brand voice, draft on-brand copy in your own voice, produce the media, assemble the card, and schedule or publish.

<Note>
Your agent drafts the copy, grounded in your real context (your outliers, brand voice, and past posts), and you approve it before anything goes live. ContentHero supplies the grounding and the method.
</Note>

## Cards, posts, and spaces

Three words carry the whole model, and they nest.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Space" icon="table-columns">
    A board. Its columns are **stages**, which you shape to your own process.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Card" icon="rectangle-list">
    One piece of work: title, script, notes, cover, and the media on it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post" icon="paper-plane">
    How a card reaches one platform. A card holds **one post per platform**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

So a card is the thing you make, and its posts are the ways it goes out. Publishing a card to Instagram and YouTube means one card with two posts.

## The five phases

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ground">
    Pull the context that makes a draft on-brand instead of generic:
    - `list_content` (scope with `brandKitId`) then `get_content` on the top few, for hook archetype, structure, pacing, and CTA style. Results are ranked by outlier score, so a small account's breakout still surfaces.
    - `get_brand_kit` for tone, vocabulary, and banned words.
    - `get_account` on one of your own tracked profiles, for how your own posts actually performed.
    - `search_brand_knowledge` for the brand's stance on the topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft (host LLM)">
    Synthesize a concept, hook, and caption that emulate the user's proven patterns in the user's own voice. The user's own top posts win over a stated profile. Present the draft for approval. Do not invent copy that is not grounded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Produce">
    Generate the media with the [generate](/guides/generate) tools. A common chain is `generate_image` for the key frame, then its output id into `generate_video` as the start frame, grounded in the brand's visual style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assemble">
    `create_card` with the approved title, then one `update_card` call carrying the caption, the `assets`, and the `posts` for each platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    `update_card` with `scheduledAt` for a future slot, or `publish_post` to go now, only after the user confirms platforms and timing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**`posts` and `assets` are declarative.** Each one replaces the whole set, so send the full list every time. Passing a single post to add a platform removes every other platform's post. Read the card first, append, then send it all back.
</Warning>

## Operations

| What | MCP tool |
| --- | --- |
| Create, read, update a card | `create_card`, `get_card`, `update_card`, `list_cards` |
| Archive a card | `archive` with `assetType: "card"` |
| Attach media | `update_card` with `assets` |
| Configure platforms | `update_card` with `posts` |
| Schedule | `update_card` with `scheduledAt` |
| Publish now | `publish_post` |
| Stages | `list_stages`, `create_stage`, `update_stage`, `delete_stage` |
| Spaces | `list_spaces`, `get_space`, `create_space`, `update_space`, `delete_space` |
| Connected accounts | `list_connected_accounts`, `get_connected_account` |

Full parameters for every one of these are in the [planner tool reference](/mcp/tools/planner).

## Connected accounts

A post publishes to a connected social account. List them with `list_connected_accounts` and pass the account id into the post's `connectedAccountId`. The encrypted credentials behind a connection are never exposed through the API; you reference an account by id.

## Approval is mandatory

<Warning>
Never schedule or publish without explicit user approval of the final content. Reads and drafts are free; publishing is gated on a yes.
</Warning>
