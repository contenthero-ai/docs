---
title: "On-brand Caption for a Reel"
description: "The everyday request: you have a clip and want a caption in your voice."
---

You have a clip and want a caption that sounds like you. Ground it, draft it, attach it, publish it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ground">
    `list_content` (scoped with `brandKitId`), then `get_content` on the top few for hooks and structure. `get_brand_kit` for voice. `get_account` on one of your own tracked profiles for your best posts. `search_brand_knowledge` for your stance on the topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Your agent writes the hook and caption emulating your proven patterns, in your voice, and presents it for approval. See [Voice synthesis](/skills/voice-synthesis).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach">
    `create_card` with the approved title, then one `update_card` carrying the caption in `notes`, the reel in `assets`, and the platform and format in `posts`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    `update_card` with `scheduledAt`, or `publish_post` to go now, after you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This is the [pipeline](/guides/pipeline) without a generation step: the media already exists, so you skip Produce.
